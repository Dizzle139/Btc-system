Each move you marked, reverse-engineered on the daily chart: the cycle it was in, the weekly FVG or order block, the EMAs, the structure (swings, BOS, CHoCH) and volume. Then the setup behind it is written as an exact rule and run on all of BTC history since 2011 to see whether it holds up.

Step 1

The five setups in your marks, as exact rules

Everything is checked at the daily close using only what was known then. Weekly values come from the last closed weekly candle. The halving clock splits the cycle into a bull phase (0–18 months and 30+ months after a halving) and a bear phase (18–30 months).

Two ways to enter each one: early = the close of the first day all three conditions are true (stop just beyond that day's low or high); confirmed = the close of the first daily CHoCH in the trade's direction within 15 days (stop beyond the lowest low or highest high since the setup day).

Step 5

Why the cycle bottoms were the bottom

You thought price hit an FVG, went through it, then bounced. The data agrees for most of the big lows: price broke down through a bullish weekly FVG (a weekly close below it, which looks like the zone failed), made the low under it, then closed a week back above it. The reclaim is the signal. The dashed green box is the zone; the lines mark the breakdown week, the low and the reclaim week.

Rule: a weekly close below the bottom of a bullish weekly FVG/OB, then within 4 weekly candles a weekly close back above that bottom. Entry at that weekly close; stop under the lowest low since the breakdown (median 15% away, because it is a weekly setup).

Step 6

What went wrong, and the fix

Every signal that failed was labelled: stop too close (stopped, then price made the move anyway within 30 days) or wrong (the move never came). One fix per setup, decided before re-running: put the stop beyond the weekly zone instead of beyond the setup day’s candle.
